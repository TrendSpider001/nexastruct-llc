Portfolio overview

Wu Dan Dan supports NexaStruct’s client-facing work by helping owners and partners understand how bundled architecture, structural, and MEP services map to their timelines and budgets. She focuses on clear scopes—what is included, what requires additional study, and what assumptions proposals rely on.

Her role connects commercial conversations to technical reality so engagements start with aligned expectations.

Clarity before contract

Misunderstood scope is expensive. Wu focuses on language and structure in proposals so deliverables and fees match the project story.

  • Owners comparing multiple fee structures and service models
  • Fast proposals that still need accurate discipline boundaries
  • Add-services triggers that should be explicit up front
  • Coordination with technical staff to validate durations

How she helps

Wu brings order to intake: questions that reveal risk early, and packaging that helps leadership respond consistently.

  • Scope templates aligned to NexaStruct integrated delivery model
  • Checklists for site, occupancy, and code drivers that affect fee
  • Proposal language reviewed for coordination responsibilities
  • Handoffs to project teams with captured client priorities
