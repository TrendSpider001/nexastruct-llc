PAUL A BERGMAN, P.E.

  • Role: Principal
  • Location: San Diego, CA
  • Credentials: Professional Engineer (P.E.)
  • Focus: Structural engineering leadership

Portfolio overview

PAUL A BERGMAN leads structural efforts where lateral systems, gravity framing, and foundation solutions must stay tightly coupled to architectural intent. She is known for readable calculations, defensible assumptions, and coordination that respects architectural moves instead of fighting them.

Her portfolio spans retrofit and new construction, with attention to constructability and phased work when buildings stay occupied.

Structural risk at speed

Structural decisions early in design echo through cost and schedule. PAUL A BERGMAN focuses on identifying governing cases quickly—so the team does not iterate blindly.

  • Seismic and wind demands against architectural massing
  • Existing conditions with incomplete as-built documentation
  • Phasing and temporary bracing in operational buildings
  • Steel versus concrete trade-offs tied to schedule and site logistics

Engineering approach

He pairs analysis rigor with communication: structural intent is expressed clearly to architects, MEP partners, and contractors.

  • Early lateral system options with pros, cons, and cost drivers
  • Coordination notes that highlight critical load paths and openings
  • Peer review mindset on unusual geometries or retrofit connections
  • Construction-phase support tuned to field questions that matter
