Frequently asked questions

1. What services does NexaStruct provide?

We provide architectural design, structural engineering, and MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and related building systems). You can engage us for all three disciplines together or for individual scopes, depending on your project.

2. Do you work design-build or design-bid-build?

Yes. We support both delivery methods and adapt our documentation and review cycles to match your contract structure and contractor involvement.

3. How do you coordinate architecture, structure, and MEP?

Our teams use shared models and regular coordination sessions so clashes are resolved before construction. A single firm culture reduces handoff delays between disciplines.

4. How do we request a proposal?

Send a short description of your project, location, schedule, and the services you need (architecture, structure, MEP, or all) via our Contact page. We will respond with next steps and a typical fee schedule.

Still have questions? We are happy to talk through your project.
